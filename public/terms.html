Flowchart AI terms

Development terms draft, October 2, 2026. These require operator approval before public plugin release.

Flowchart AI creates and stores link-shared diagrams. Save only material you are authorized to share. Anyone with the view link can read the chart; anyone with its private edit link can edit or delete it. Charts have no account ownership guarantees or recovery of lost edit capabilities. Do not include confidential information or credentials.

Charts remain indefinitely unless server retention is configured or an edit-link holder deletes them. Deletion removes the active record and invalidates links, but cannot erase copies held by others or material already in chat. Review the privacy draft for the data flow.

This development service is provided as available, with rate and storage limits. Do not rely on it as your only copy of important work; export a copy from the browser. Availability and continued retention are not guaranteed. Do not abuse the service or attempt to access or change diagrams without permission.

The plugin's deterministic tools do not invoke an LLM. The browser AI assistant is a separate service. Installation does not grant Sign in with ChatGPT or access to a user's ChatGPT inference allowance. Optional Premium billing and image generation use a separate private browser billing session and configured payment/inference providers. This development implementation does not establish published commercial subscription terms.

Source code is provided under its repository license. These draft service terms do not change that license. General project information is available in the repository.
